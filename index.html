Methodology & Ethics · How I Use AI

How I Use AI

My ethical framework, collaborative boundaries, and public audit log for incorporating artificial intelligence into digital humanities scholarship.

I · Inquiry

Critical Inquiry

AI is treated as a probabilistic sounding board and experimental foil, never an authoritative oracle. Every factual assertion, citation, and suggestion is rigorously cross-examined against primary sources.

II · Attribution

Full Attribution

Every AI interaction is documented. Prompts, model versions, suggestions, and the specific human modifications made to synthetic text or code are publicly recorded.

III · Agency

Human Authorship

Core arguments, ethical positions, aesthetic decisions, and personal reflections originate strictly with the human author. AI may assist with syntax or brainstorming, but never authorial voice.

IV · Ecology

Material Awareness

Continuous awareness of the physical infrastructure, water cooling, energy expenditure, and global human annotation labor required to sustain foundation models.
