# How I Use AI: Methodology & Principles

In DH 101, artificial intelligence is neither treated as an infallible oracle nor as a shortcut to bypass original thought. Instead, I treat generative AI as an **experimental research instrument, critical foil, and interlocutor** that must be rigorously audited, contextualized, and cited.

---

## The Four Core Principles

### 1. Critical Inquiry Over Passive Acceptance
I approach AI outputs with healthy skepticism. Large language models and generative systems are pattern-completion machines; they synthesize probabilistic representations rather than understanding truth or human experience. When consulting an AI:
- I fact-check all empirical claims, bibliographies, and historical assertions against authoritative primary and secondary sources.
- I analyze what assumptions, biases, and cultural norms the model embeds in its prose or visual generations.

### 2. Full Attribution & Auditable Logs
Transparency is essential to scholarly integrity. Every instance of AI assistance in this portfolio is accompanied by:
- **The specific tool and model** (e.g., Claude 3.5 Sonnet, GPT-4o, Google Gemini, Midjourney).
- **The exact prompt** and rhetorical framing utilized.
- **The specific delta**: clearly distinguishing what was machine-generated from what was authored, edited, restructured, or refuted by me.

### 3. Primacy of Human Voice & Intellectual Ownership
The central thesis, critical analysis, personal voice, and ethical conclusions of every make and reflection are entirely my own. 
- AI is never used to write reflections on personal experiences or ethical values.
- In coding tasks, AI may be used for syntax debugging, regex creation, or architectural suggestions, but every line of code is understood, adapted, and tested by me before inclusion.

### 4. Continuous Reflection on Power & Ecology
Every computational project prompts meta-reflection:
- *Why did I choose to use an automated system for this task?*
- *What labor and environmental costs were incurred in generating this response?*
- *Could this project have been realized through traditional humanistic close-reading or hand-crafting?*

## Typical Collaborative Scenarios

| Stage of Work | How AI is Used | Human Guardrails & Verification |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ideation & Brainstorming** | Prompting for divergent perspectives, opposing arguments, or unexpected analogies. | Filtering out cliches; selecting only conceptually rigorous directions. |
| **Code Troubleshooting** | Explaining unfamiliar error messages or suggesting CSS layout alternatives. | Understanding why the error occurred; reviewing and refining code for accessibility. |
| **Text Analysis & Distant Reading** | Generating Python parsing scripts, exploring token distributions. | Verifying data integrity, validating token counts against raw corpus. |
| **Attribution & Logging** | Documenting the interaction lifecycle in the course `ai-log`. | Providing honest rationale for what was kept, discarded, or challenged. |
